# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone taking part in OnlyBackup, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

Expected behavior:

- Be considerate and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information, or your own secrets, in issues or code.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions that do not follow this code, and may block repeat offenders from the repository.

To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [dennidalpos](https://github.com/dennidalpos). Reports are handled privately. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
